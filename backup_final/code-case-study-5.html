Churn Prediction Model

Classification Model for User Retention

📊 Business Challenge

Premium users are churning, but retention offers are expensive (£5/user). We need to target only those truly at risk who are worth saving.

🎯 Optimization Result

Model optimized for Profit, not just Accuracy. Reduced total cost of churn + retention spend by 18% compared to blanket offers.
