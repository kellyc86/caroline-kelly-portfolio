Event Discovery Gap Analysis

Market Sizing & Opportunity Analysis

📊 Business Question

Where are users searching for events but not finding them? Quantify underserved markets where demand exceeds supply.

🎯 Key Finding

Identified £2.1M annual TAM in underserved searches. Manchester Alternative scene shows highest gap (8,200 searches, only 45 events) — opportunity for 150+ additional listings.

💡 Analysis Approach

  1. Search Data Collection: Aggregate user searches by city-genre combination
  2. Supply Mapping: Count available events in each segment
  3. Gap Calculation: Identify discrepancies vs. platform average coverage
  4. Market Sizing: Convert gaps into addressable revenue opportunities
  5. Priority Ranking: Score opportunities by TAM, competitiveness, and entry barriers
