Where should the next hour of selling go?

Where should the next hour of selling go?

A weekly refreshed list of 60 ranked companies inside Salesforce, each carrying the evidence behind its rank, plus a model of which open deals will close. MSc dissertation with Williams' commercial team, deployed on their infrastructure and refreshed weekly.

84%
of future sponsors placed in the top decile before their deals were announced; 13.7% precision against a 1.6% base rate.

Does genre live in the audio?

Five classifiers and an autoencoder ablation on 10,000 tracks. Raw features beat the learned representation; popularity beat every audio feature; pop, indie and hip-hop are a positioning choice.
