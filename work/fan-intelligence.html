What do a label's fans actually want?

A pipeline that gives a record label a structured, queryable view of fan behaviour across five social media and streaming platforms, served through REST, WebSocket and MCP.

RoleSole builder, individual coursework
StackPython, PyArrow, Apache Iceberg, DuckDB, MongoDB Atlas, PostgreSQL 15, Snorkel, MLflow, LangChain, Llama 3 via Ollama, FastAPI, Docker, Terraform
DataReddit, YouTube, Spotify, Last.fm and Fandom Wiki for five artists; 4,827 fans
CodePrivate repository (33 commits, 57 tests). Walkthrough on request.
Independent coursework built around a UMG-style brief. Not commissioned by Universal Music Group.

Problem

What share of an artist's community are Hardcore, Parasocial, Critical or Casual fans? Merchandise, fan club pricing and tour allocation depend on that split, not on whether the community feels positive.

Five platforms, no common format, changing APIs, tight rate limits: getting them into one queryable place is data engineering before it is analytics.

Skills

  • Medallion architecture: Bronze JSON, Iceberg/Parquet Silver with a 12-field schema, DuckDB Gold views
  • Weak supervision: 12 Snorkel labelling functions, four archetypes
  • Matrix factorisation: TruncatedSVD on a fans-by-artists matrix
  • RAG agent: LangChain ReAct, five tools, Atlas embeddings, Presidio PII redaction
  • Lineage: W3C PROV and OpenLineage to Marquez at every stage
  • DevOps: seven Docker services, Terraform, GitHub Actions CI

Results

67.2%of variance in 4,827 fans in three factors: breadth across artists (26.2%) and depth within one artist (20.9%), driven by heavy Last.fm listeners
90 to 94%positive sentiment on all five artists: too uniform to be a finding about fans, so read as a data-quality signal
89%Casual: the archetype split an empty Silver write produced without error. The first distribution reported was wrong, and the Bronze check exists because of it

Pipeline

1 · CollectRaw API responses to Bronze.
2 · SilverTyped schema, three checks per write.
3 · GoldFive DuckDB views over Silver Parquet.
4 · ModelSnorkel archetypes, SVD, RoBERTa sentiment, MLflow.
5 · AgentReAct over vector search, local Llama 3.
6 · ServeFastAPI REST, WebSocket, MCP.

What a label would do with this

Run the archetype split per artist community before a release: a community that is 34% Hardcore and 12% Parasocial gets a different deluxe edition, fan club price and tour allocation from one that is mostly Casual. The breadth and depth factors separate superfans of one act from broad listeners, which is what superfan identification needs.

Limits. Silent failures are more dangerous than loud ones: nothing complained about the empty Silver write until validation was added. The uniform positive sentiment likely reflects subreddit self-selection. Recall@k of 0.985 is omitted: with five artists and k of at least 5 it is near 1 by construction. Llama 3 on CPU takes 8 to 15 seconds a query.
