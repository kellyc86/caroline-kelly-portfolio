What do a label's fans actually want?

A pipeline that gives a record label a structured, queryable view of fan behaviour across five social media and streaming platforms, served through REST, WebSocket and MCP.

RoleSole builder, individual coursework
StackPython, PyArrow, Apache Iceberg, DuckDB, MongoDB Atlas, PostgreSQL 15, Snorkel, MLflow, LangChain, Llama 3 via Ollama, FastAPI, Docker, Terraform
DataReddit, YouTube, Spotify, Last.fm and Fandom Wiki for five artists; 4,827 fans
CodePrivate repository (33 commits, 57 tests). Walkthrough on request.
Independent coursework built around a UMG-style brief. Not commissioned by Universal Music Group.

Problem

Fan communities live on platforms that share no common format, change their APIs without warning, and have rate limits designed for casual developers rather than bulk analytical pipelines. Building something coherent out of that mess is a data engineering problem before it is an analytics one.

The goal is a system whose internal logic scales cleanly, not one that works for five artists and falls apart at five hundred.

Skills

  • Medallion architecture: immutable Bronze JSON, Iceberg/Parquet Silver under a 12-field PyArrow schema, DuckDB Gold views
  • Weak supervision: 12 Snorkel labelling functions across four archetypes: Casual, Hardcore, Parasocial, Critical
  • Matrix factorisation: TruncatedSVD on a fans-by-artists engagement matrix
  • RAG agent: LangChain ReAct with five tools, 384-dim embeddings in MongoDB Atlas, Presidio PII redaction
  • Lineage: W3C PROV documents and OpenLineage events to Marquez at every stage boundary
  • DevOps: seven Docker services, Terraform for S3, RDS and ECR, GitHub Actions CI

Results

67.2%of variance explained by three latent factors across 4,827 fans
0.985Recall@k, stable across all cut-offs; NDCG@k 0.767
5engineering defects found, fixed and documented, including a silent empty Silver write

Pipeline

1 · CollectFive collectors write every API response to Bronze exactly as received.
2 · SilverTyped PyArrow schema, SHA-256 text hashes, three validation checks on every write.
3 · GoldFive DuckDB views directly over Silver Parquet.
4 · ModelSnorkel archetypes, SVD factors, RoBERTa sentiment, registered in MLflow.
5 · AgentReAct agent over Atlas vector search and a local Llama 3.
6 · ServeFastAPI REST, WebSocket stream and MCP endpoint.
Limits. Silent failures are more dangerous than loud ones: an empty Silver write produced an archetype distribution that collapsed to 89% Casual and nothing complained until a Bronze existence check was added. Positive sentiment at 90 to 94 per cent across all five artists demands scepticism and likely reflects subreddit self-selection. Llama 3 on CPU takes 8 to 15 seconds per query.
